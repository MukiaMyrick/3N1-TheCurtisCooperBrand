# 3N1 Calendly booking integration

The four booking links supplied by the business have been connected to booking.html:

- Barber: https://calendly.com/teamcurtis31/3n1-barber
- Real Estate & Investing: https://calendly.com/teamcurtis31/3n1-real-estate-investing-consulting
- DJ/Entertainment: https://calendly.com/teamcurtis31/3n1-dj-entertainment
- Business & Entrepreneur Consulting: https://calendly.com/teamcurtis31/business-entrepreneur-consulting

The booking page embeds each Calendly event in an iframe and offers a direct-link fallback. Appointment availability, confirmation emails, reminders, and calendar conflict checks depend on settings in the business's Calendly account. This website does not itself send confirmations or synchronize the CRM. Test each event link and booking flow before publishing.
